# Ethics and scope

This framework is for historical subjects, public figures acting in their public role, and public topics, researched from **published sources**.

It is **not** for compiling dossiers on private individuals: no home addresses, contacts, family details, private social media, leaked or breached data, or anything that could enable stalking, harassment or doxxing. For public figures, stick to their public record and published reporting. A dossier may include *published* personal history (parents, marriages, children, residences, illness, controversies), but only as follows:

- Give residences by city or region, never street addresses.
- Keep living relatives to names and basics that reputable outlets have already published.
- Report controversies neutrally, quoting reputable reporting directly. Don't editorialise.
- Label self-reported figures (money, dates, counts) as the subject's own account.
- Leave out rumors, gossip and unsourced fan claims.

Copyright: mirror only public-domain or openly licensed texts and record the license. Link copyrighted scholarship; quote briefly at most.
