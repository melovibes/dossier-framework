# Ethics and scope

This framework is for historical subjects, public figures acting in their public role, and public topics, researched from **published sources**.

It is **not** for compiling dossiers on private individuals: no home addresses, contacts, family details, private social media, leaked or breached data, or anything that could enable stalking, harassment or doxxing. For living public figures, stick to their public record and published reporting.

Copyright: mirror only public-domain or openly licensed texts and record the license. Link copyrighted scholarship; quote briefly at most.
