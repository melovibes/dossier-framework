C01 · Hypatia was killed by a Christian mob in 415

Strong historical conclusion argued in 03 — What is disputed, and why?

Assessment: Two independent ancient accounts, one Christian (S01) and one pagan (S02), agree that she was killed by a Christian crowd in Alexandria in Cyril's time; S01 dates it to 415.

Evidence for: S01 S02

Evidence against / complications: Motives and Cyril's responsibility are disputed; S02 is hostile to Cyril.

What would change the grade: A new independent source dating the death differently.
