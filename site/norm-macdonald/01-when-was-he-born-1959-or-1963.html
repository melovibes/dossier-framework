01 — When was he born: 1959 or 1963?

Every major obituary gives October 17, 1959, in Quebec City S01 S02 S05. The confusion is his own doing: for years he gave out 1963, and the Globe and Mail says he "insisted" on it S02. That figure got into the record. EW called him 34 in January 1998, which only works for 1963 S07, and The Canadian Encyclopedia still prints 1963 S03. CBC first ran 1963 in its obituary and then published a correction to 1959 S01.

The 1959 date is graded strong C01 and 1963 false C02. This is a case where a public figure's own statements are the weakest evidence about him.
