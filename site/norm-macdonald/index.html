Executive findings — Norm Macdonald

Norm Macdonald (1959–2021) was a Canadian stand-up comedian, best known as SNL's Weekend Update anchor from 1994 to 1997, whose deadpan style made him a comedian's comedian S01 S02. The record of his public career is dense and consistent: his start in Canadian clubs, SNL, Dirty Work, The Norm Show, the podcast, the final Letterman set, and his death from leukemia in 2021 S01 S03 S06.

The weak points are mostly ones he made himself. He spent years giving a false birth year S02. He wrote a "memoir" that is mostly invented S16. And he concealed his illness S01. The main contested question, why NBC pulled him off Weekend Update, stays open: the O.J. explanation is likely but has never been confirmed by the man who made the call S08 S09 S10.
