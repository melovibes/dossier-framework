04 — Films and TV: Dirty Work, The Norm Show and after

His first film lead came in Dirty Work (1998). He co-wrote it with Frank Sebastiano and Fred Wolf, and Bob Saget directed. Variety called it "sporadically hilarious" and uneven S12 C10. He then co-created and starred in The Norm Show on ABC (1999–2001), playing a former NHL player sentenced to community service as a social worker S01 S03 C11. Later credits include A Minute with Stan Hooper (2003), Sports Show with Norm Macdonald (2011), Norm Macdonald Has a Show on Netflix (2018), and a recurring role on The Middle S06. The pattern is short runs, which the A.V. Club tied partly to his refusal to play the industry game S16.

Film and voice work

He voiced Lucky, the dog, in Dr. Dolittle (1998) and its sequels S47 S46, and starred with Dave Chappelle in Screwed (2000), which Variety panned and in which it found him "stiff and uncomfortable" S48 C45. Late in life he voiced Yaphit on The Orville, recording a final session from hospital S46 S52.

Stand-up specials

His hour specials were Me Doing Standup (Comedy Central, 2011) and Hitler's Dog, Gossip & Trickery (Netflix, 2017); Vice called the latter his first special in six years and portrayed him as indifferent to topical relevance S37 C38. Nothing Special (2022) is covered in chapter 06.

Norm Macdonald Has a Show (Netflix, 2018)

The Netflix talk show dropped the monologue and band; the WSJ described it as guest conversation with no political commentary. Guests included David Letterman and Jane Fonda S39. It premiered on September 14, 2018, days after the controversy described in chapter 12 S30. Vulture's review was largely negative S38, and it ran for a single season C39.
