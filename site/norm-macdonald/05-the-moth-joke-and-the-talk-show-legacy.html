05 — The moth joke and the talk-show legacy

His talk-show reputation rests on long, deliberately anti-climactic stories. The best known is the "moth joke," told on The Tonight Show with Conan O'Brien in 2009. It runs for minutes before landing on the punchline "'cause the light was on" S23. He thought enough of it to reprint it in his memoir S15 S16 C12. O'Brien has said Macdonald got the joke from Colin Quinn S23.

On May 15, 2015, he was the last stand-up on David Letterman's Late Show. He broke down at the end and said, "If something is true, it is not sentimental… I love you" S14 S01 C13.
