03 — SNL, Weekend Update and the 1998 removal

He joined SNL in 1993 and anchored Weekend Update from 1994 to 1997 S01 S03 C07. His O.J. Simpson jokes treated Simpson's guilt as settled fact, and after the 1995 acquittal he said "murder is legal in the state of California" S01 S11.

In January 1998, NBC West Coast president Don Ohlmeyer removed him from Update and replaced him with Colin Quinn, 72 hours before the first new show of the year S07 S08 C08. Why he was removed is the dossier's main dispute C09:

  • The O.J. explanation. Head writer Jim Downey, who was also fired, says outright that Ohlmeyer, a Simpson friend of decades, "had just had enough" S09. Most obituaries repeat this S01.
  • Ohlmeyer's explanation. In 1998 he told TV critics that the segment had plateaued and that he "resented the inference" S08. In a 2004 Television Academy interview he blamed ratings and dismissed the O.J. idea S10. The Deseret News pointed out that he never actually denied the reports S08.
  • Macdonald's own account. At the time he said "Ohlmeyer told me he didn't find me funny" S07. Later he put it down to general insubordination S11. EW also reported a possible trigger: Ohlmeyer's anger that Macdonald would host the ESPYs on a rival network S07.

Verdict: the O.J. jokes almost certainly played a part, but sole causation is unproved.

On-air persona: impressions and running bits

Beyond Update he was known for impressions, among them Burt Reynolds on Celebrity Jeopardy!, David Letterman, Larry King and Bob Dole S46 S53 S01 C43. His Update segments had recurring devices, such as pulling out a pocket tape recorder for a deadpan "Note to self" aside S57 (C44; fan transcript only, so graded probable).

ESPYs and the 1999 return as host

He hosted the ESPY Awards in February 1998 S42 S07 C41, the gig EW linked to Ohlmeyer's irritation. On October 23, 1999, less than two years after his removal from Update, he returned to host SNL S50. His monologue opened with the firing itself: "They fired me because they said that I wasn't funny" S43 C42.
