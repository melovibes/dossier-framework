10 — Personal life and family

This chapter records only what reputable outlets have published. In keeping with ETHICS.md it gives cities or regions, never addresses, and keeps detail about living relatives (his son, ex-wife and brothers) to names and publicly reported basics.

Parents and siblings

His father, Percy Lloyd Macdonald, was vice-principal at the school on CFB Valcartier, north of Quebec City, where his mother, Ferne (née Mains), also taught S02. Percy died of heart disease in 1990 S25. Norm was the middle of three brothers: Neil, the older, became a CBC parliamentary and foreign correspondent, and Leslie is the younger S02 S28 S01 C25. His mother outlived him; in 2016 she was reported to split her time between Canada and Los Angeles S25 S02.

Marriage and divorce

He married Connie Vaillancourt in 1988; they had one son and divorced 11 years later S04 C22. Rolling Stone reported him separated in April 1999 S26. By 2016 he had been married only once and would say little about his ex-wife, a marriage therapist in Los Angeles, beyond "she's great" and "a fine person" S25 C23.

Children

His only child, Dylan, was born in the early 1990s: Rolling Stone gave his age as 6 in April 1999 and the Washington Post as 23 in August 2016 S26 S25. Dylan survived him and is named in the obituaries S04 S02 C24. Neil Macdonald said Norm's secrecy about his illness was above all to protect his son S02.

Dating and relationships

No reputable source checked reports a romantic partner after the 1999 separation (C30, unproved). Profiles from 2016 and 2018 describe a private man who rarely went out S25 S27. Online fan speculation about specific people is not evidence and is deliberately not repeated here.
