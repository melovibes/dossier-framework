# C29 — He died in the Los Angeles area; reports differ on the exact city.

**Assessment:** CBC (quoting his brother) says Los Angeles; the Toronto Star says Pasadena. Some reference works give Duarte, home of the City of Hope hospital, but no primary or major-outlet source checked here confirms it.

**Evidence for:** [S01] [S29]
