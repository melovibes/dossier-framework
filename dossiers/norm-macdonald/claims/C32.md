# C32 — By his own account he gambled away everything he had more than once.

**Assessment:** The 2016 Washington Post profile states he 'gambled away everything — twice'; GQ quotes him: 'I know what it's like to lose a dollar when you're broke, because I've been broke.' Both rest on what he told reporters.

**Evidence for:** [S25] [S33]

**Evidence against / complications:** He gave different counts in different interviews (twice in 2016; fan sites cite 'three times' from later podcasts, not verified here). No figures are independently documented.

**What would change the grade:** Financial or court records.
