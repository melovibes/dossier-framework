# C09 — He was removed from Weekend Update because of his jokes about O.J. Simpson, a friend of Ohlmeyer's.

**Assessment:** Head writer Jim Downey says so directly; most obituaries repeat it.

**Evidence for:** [S09] [S08] [S10] [S11] [S07]

**Evidence against / complications:** Ohlmeyer denied it in 1998 and 2004 and cited ratings and a plateau; Macdonald himself said Ohlmeyer 'didn't find me funny' and later blamed insubordination. EW reported an ESPYs-hosting trigger.

**What would change the grade:** Ohlmeyer's own records, or testimony from Lorne Michaels.
