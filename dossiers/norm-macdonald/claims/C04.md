# C04 — He began stand-up at an amateur night at Yuk Yuk's in Ottawa in 1985.

**Assessment:** The Globe gives the date and venue; CBC confirms he started touring Canadian clubs.

**Evidence for:** [S02] [S01]

**Evidence against / complications:** The 1985 date rests mainly on one obituary.

**What would change the grade:** An interview or club record giving another year.
