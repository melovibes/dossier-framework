# C27 — He first moved to Los Angeles around 1992, before joining SNL.

**Assessment:** The NYT says that in 1992, after touring Canadian clubs, he moved to Los Angeles, where he wrote for The Dennis Miller Show and Roseanne.

**Evidence for:** [S27]

**Evidence against / complications:** Single source. A CNN-syndicated obituary ties the move to a 1987 LA festival visit without a date, so the exact year is soft.

**What would change the grade:** A second dated source.
