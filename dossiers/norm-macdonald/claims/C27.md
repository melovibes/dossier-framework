# C27 — He first moved to Los Angeles around 1992, before joining SNL.

**Evidence for:** [S27] [S53]

**Evidence against / complications:** CNN [S53] also has him moving to LA before Roseanne (1992), but ties it to a 1987 festival visit without dating the move; the exact year remains soft.

**What would change the grade:** A second dated source.
