**Assessment:** The Washington Post's 2022 reconstruction, sourced to Lori Jo Hoekstra, gives the 2013 diagnosis, the four-month Arizona transplant, the 2020 progression and a third transplant in March 2021; the Globe independently dates the diagnosis to 2013.
