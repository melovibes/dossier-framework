# C23 — He was married only once, and he did not discuss his ex-wife publicly beyond saying she was 'great' and a 'fine person'.

**Assessment:** Edgers (2016) states he had been married once; the LA Times obituary records no other marriage.

**Evidence for:** [S25] [S04]

**Evidence against / complications:** No source after 2016 addresses whether he remarried; none of the 2021 obituaries mentions a later spouse.

**What would change the grade:** A reliable report of a later marriage.
