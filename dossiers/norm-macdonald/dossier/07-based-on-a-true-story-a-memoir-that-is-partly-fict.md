# 07 — Based on a True Story: a memoir that is partly fiction

Based on a True Story (2016) is sold as a memoir but reads as a comic novel. It has a copyright-page disclaimer about his "spotty" memory, an imaginary ghostwriter, and an invented road trip to pay off gambling debts with Adam Eget [S15] [S16]. Publishers Weekly called it a "faux memoir" [S17] (C15). Episodes such as buying his SNL job with morphine for Lorne Michaels, and wagering his soul with the devil, are deliberate fabrications (C16) [S15] [S16].

What reviewers treat as real: his long struggle with gambling, and his acceptance that he would be remembered for SNL [S16] [S02]. Rule for this dossier: nothing from the memoir counts as evidence unless an independent source confirms it.
