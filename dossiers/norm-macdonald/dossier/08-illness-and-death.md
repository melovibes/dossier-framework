# 08 — Illness and death

He died on September 14, 2021, at 61. His brother Neil told CBC the cause was leukemia, diagnosed "a long time ago," and that he kept it quiet so it wouldn't affect his comedy [S01] (C17). The LA Times and BBC say he had the illness for nine years [S04] [S05], while the Globe dates the diagnosis to 2013 [S02] (C18, disputed). He was still booked for the New York Comedy Festival that November [S01]. This dossier goes no further into medical detail than those obituaries; published family history is in chapter 10.
