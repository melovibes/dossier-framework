# 13 — Faith

He described himself as a Christian. On Larry King's show he said: "I'm a Christian. It's not stylish to say that, now" [S34]. He raised theology with guests, for instance asking Jane Fonda about Christ's nature on his Netflix show [S34], and sometimes tweeted about it [S34] (C37). After his death the New York Times ran an opinion essay arguing that his comedy was "quite Christian" and observing that most obituaries ignored his faith [S35].

First Things offered a counterweight, saying he "counted himself, in a vague way, as a Christian" and was not an apologist [S36]. He gave no public denominational detail, and this dossier goes no further than his own statements.
