# 11 — Gambling and poker

Gambling ran through his adult life. The 2016 Washington Post profile summed it up: "He doesn't drink or do drugs. But he gambled away everything — twice" [S25]. Reviews of his memoir and a 2006 reporting trip by GQ describe the same pattern of craps, blackjack, sports betting and poker [S16] [S33] (C31).

**His own account.** The amounts and the number of times he went broke come from Macdonald himself. He told GQ he once won about $75,000 at craps at the Trump Taj Mahal in Atlantic City while he was on SNL, and said: "I know what it's like to lose a dollar when you're broke, because I've been broke" [S33] (C33, unproved, self-reported). The Post says twice; later interviews are often quoted as saying three times. This dossier treats the count as his own and unverified (C32). A frequently shared "$400,000 three times" quote is actually Dana White describing himself and is not used.

**Poker on record.** On Bravo's Celebrity Poker Showdown (Season 2, aired June 10, 2004), he knocked out Adam Rodriguez on the first hand, the only time that happened on the show, then lost heads-up to Michael Ian Black [S44] [S45] (C34). The Hendon Mob also records a 20th-place finish in a 2007 World Series of Poker event [S45].
