# 05 — The moth joke and the talk-show legacy

His talk-show reputation rests on long, deliberately anti-climactic stories. The best known is the "moth joke," told on The Tonight Show with Conan O'Brien in 2009. It runs for minutes before landing on the punchline "'cause the light was on" [S23]. He thought enough of it to reprint it in his memoir [S15] [S16], and the New Yorker called it the purest instance of his skill at stringing out a joke [S51] (C12). O'Brien has said Macdonald got the joke from Colin Quinn [S23].

On May 15, 2015, he was the last stand-up on David Letterman's Late Show. He broke down at the end and said, "If something is true, it is not sentimental… I love you" [S14] [S01] (C13).

## The Saget roast (2008)

At Comedy Central's 2008 roast of Bob Saget, he skipped insults and read a string of deliberately corny old jokes off index cards (for example, "like a flower — yeah, a cauliflower"), baffling the room before ending with a sincere tribute to Saget [S40]. Saget later said Macdonald had told him "I can't say mean things about you because you're my friend," and that he'd planned to read from an old joke book [S41]. (C40).

## Talk-show guest

He was a favourite guest of Letterman and O'Brien [S01] [S23]. No authoritative count of his appearances was found, so no "record" claim is made here.

## Twitter

He used Twitter for long, deadpan play-by-play of golf tournaments, which Slate called "boring genius," and often deleted the tweets afterwards [S49]. David Spade described hours of commentary on individual putts [S25] (C46).
