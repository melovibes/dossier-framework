# S02 — Suda, s.v. Hypatia, preserving Damascius

**What it is:** The Byzantine Suda's entry on Hypatia, which preserves the account of the pagan philosopher Damascius, who blamed her murder on Bishop Cyril's supporters. The Suda On Line translation is searchable from the linked home page.

**Distance from the subject:** Damascius wrote about 115 years later; independent of Socrates (S01) and hostile to Cyril.

**Reliability:** B (later and partisan, but independent of S01).
